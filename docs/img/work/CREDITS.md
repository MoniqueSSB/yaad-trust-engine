# Photos of real work, for the marketing site

Chosen 15 October 2026, on the founder's brief: real photographs, not AI; every worker shown is Black; every worker is in workwear or protective gear. Location does not matter. Seven are from Pexels; the plumbing and electrical photos are from Wikimedia Commons and resized to 900px on the long side.

**Why these are safe to use.** The Pexels licence (pexels.com/license) allows use on websites, apps, ads and marketing, allows editing, and does not require credit. Pexels' upload rules require the uploader to be the photographer and ban generative AI content. Each photo was checked by hand as well: eight carry the camera make, model and the moment the shutter fired. The one without camera data was uploaded in June 2021 by Kindel Media, a known studio, before photo-realistic AI image tools were public.

**Two photos have their own licence.** The plumbing and electrical photos come from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 4.0, not the Pexels licence. Each must carry a visible credit with the author, a link to the licence, and a note that it was resized. That line sits under the What we cover tiles on marketplace.html. Do not remove it, and do not use either photo anywhere without the same credit.

**Two limits.** Never show these people in a bad light, and never caption a stock photo as a Yaadly job or worker. Every placement on the site says so in a small line underneath. Swap in real pilot job photos once there are some.
