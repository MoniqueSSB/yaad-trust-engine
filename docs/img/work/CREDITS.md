# Photos of real work, for the marketing site

Added 15 October 2026. Every file in this folder is a real camera photograph from Pexels, resized to 900px on the long side so pages stay fast.

**Why these are safe to use.** The Pexels licence (pexels.com/license) allows use on websites, apps, ads and marketing, allows editing, and does not require attribution. Pexels' upload rules require the uploader to be the photographer and state "no generative AI content". Each photo below was checked by hand: the Pexels page carries camera make and model and the date the shutter fired, which AI images do not have. Where Pexels records a location it is listed.

**Two limits.** Do not show any of these people in a bad light, and do not imply they endorse Yaadly (no "one of our workers" captions on a stock photo). None of these were taken in Jamaica: there are no free, non-AI photos of Jamaican tradespeople at work on Pexels, Unsplash, Wikimedia Commons, Flickr or Openverse as of today. The ones chosen are the closest match: Black tradespeople, block construction, zinc sheeting, tropical light.
