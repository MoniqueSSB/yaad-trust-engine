Who comes to your property

Nobody attends unverified.

🪪 Identity, checked properly

Government photo ID checked for authenticity by an independent verification provider, with a live selfie and liveness detection. An upload proves somebody has a document. A live check proves the person holding it is them.

👮 Police check where it matters

A Jamaica Constabulary Force record check is mandatory on any job over £500, on work inside an occupied home, and any time a worker holds keys or attends an empty property. Below that, with you present, you can still require it at no cost.

📞 References spoken to

Past work reviewed and referees spoken to directly before a worker is trusted with the bigger jobs, plus a trial job with an independent reviewer on site at Yaadly's cost. Certified trades are checked with the body that issued the certificate.

🔁 And it holds afterwards

We only keep working with people who meet the evidence standard. Fall short once and we stop sending work. You are told who is coming before they arrive.

The money

Never more than a quarter, before anything exists.

Never a deposit above 25%, ideally nothing up front or only enough to cover materials. Stage payments tied to milestones you can photograph, not to dates. Never the final instalment until you have inspected the work. Materials are a pass-through at cost and are never marked up.

💷
Said plainly: online payment through Yaadly is not switched on yet. Today you pay your tradesperson directly, stage by stage, against an agreement you both sign first, and our fee is invoiced separately after the work. The whole route your money takes, written down.
