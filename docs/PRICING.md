# Yaadly pricing — decided 26 August 2026

Every figure has a published UK market comparable. Sources at the bottom.
Supersedes the inspection-ladder line in `Yaadly_Pricing_Review.md` §A (30 Jul 2026).

## The principle

Built to the shape the UK market already uses, because that is the market diaspora
clients benchmark against.

**A checker records what is there. A trained inspector tells you what it means.**
That is the RICS Level 1 → Level 2 line, and it is where the price steps.

Full prices are published everywhere. Founding prices for the first five sit below them
as a **real, dated discount off a price that is genuinely purchasable** — required under
**DMCCA 2024 s226**, where a reference price nobody can actually buy is a misleading action.

**Yaadly does not price work.** No band is shown to a worker, ever. A band a worker can
see is a band a worker quotes to, and publishing one is price estimation — the one thing
the founder rule excludes.

## Add-on

**Live viewing — £40, on any rung.** By default the inspection happens and the client gets
the report and sign-off afterwards. If they would rather be there, a video call is opened
from site: they see what the inspector sees, can ask for a closer look while the inspector
is still standing there, and anything they raise goes in the report as their own note.

## Marketplace fee — unchanged

**27% blended on labour only, never on materials.** Client 15% Guarantee & Support fee,
worker 12%, worker keeps **88%**. Yaadly Remote (digital) 10%. Small-job client minimum
J$3,500–4,500 by trade risk. Materials cash advance 5%. FX ~2%.

Under **DMCCA 2024 s230 / CMA209 para 4.4** the client fee must sit **inside the headline
price**, not be added at checkout. (AA was fined £4.2m over a £3 booking fee.)

## What was wrong before, and how

**£40–60 for a site attendance** sat *below a US 4-point insurance check* ($50–100) and at
roughly **one sixth** of the cheapest UK re-inspection (£290). The only comparables that low
are Nigerian services on a Nigerian cost base — NaijaSight ≈£74, SimpleXD ≈£27. Yaadly Ltd
is a UK company carrying UK professional indemnity cover with territorial reach into Jamaica
and jurisdiction including the USA and Canada. Their prices are right for their cost base.

**Margin, before:** at £40–60 client price with the looker paid £25–35, gross was **£5–35 a
visit** before travel. At £149 with the looker on £25–35 it is ~78%, leaving room to pay
lookers £45–60 and still clear 60% — which is the "better pay = quality people" logic the
pricing book already argues for.

**The retainer was priced below the sum of its own visits.** Two visits at £149 are £298;
£350 charged £52 for the monthly report, the accountability call, the WhatsApp line and
carrying the job for a month.

**Three names, one product.** Property Check In (£149) + Eyes On It (£95/£125) + "visual
reviewer" were the same visit at three prices — now Visual Check. Site Verification Visit
(£249) + Property Condition Report (£249/£349) were the same report — now Condition Report.

## Open decisions

1. **A3 Full Report with advice, £595** — no founder decision behind it. Confirm or cut.
2. **Property Care £95 vs the £85 floor.** Recurring care sits outside the one-off ladder.
   Either it holds at £95 with the local J$ menu untouched, or the floor is written to
   apply only to one-off reviews.
3. **Fee stacking on Full PM** — 12–15% against a UK norm of 8–15% for all professional
   fees combined.
4. **Deposit Check headroom** — Service Menu v1 recommended £199 after the first five;
   QS early advice starts at £300. Room exists, no urgency.
