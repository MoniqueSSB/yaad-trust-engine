Straight answers

Questions people actually ask.

How do I start a job?
Message us on WhatsApp with where the property is and what needs doing, in text, photos or a voice note, Patois or English. Or use the form on this page. We reply within 24 hours with next steps. No app to download, no forms to fight.
Where do you operate?
Yaadly is built for property work across Jamaica. We are launching in Kingston and Portmore first, with professional oversight on every job. Starting deep in one area is deliberate: it is how we keep every job verified rather than spreading thin. More parishes open as we grow. Elsewhere in Jamaica today? Message us anyway: professional services travel further than repairs, and you will be first to know when we reach your parish.
How does the money side work?
Your payment is held and released only when you approve the evidence. The worker sees the money is real before starting, you see timestamped proof before anything is released. Nothing moves without your say so. Online holding goes live once our professional indemnity insurance is in force; until then the terms are agreed with you directly in writing before work starts.
What does it cost?
Prices are published when we open, which happens once our professional indemnity insurance is in force. We are not taking payment before then, on purpose. Everything will be quoted in writing before any work begins. Join the waiting list and you will be told the day booking opens. Property work gets a written quote benchmarked against real material costs and real day rates, confirmed before any money moves. No surprises after the fact, and no "farrin price": a client overseas pays what a client in Jamaica pays.
What do I actually get at the end of a job?
A signed Completion Report, as a PDF that is yours to keep: the job summary, before and after photographs side by side, the verification record, the worker's own confirmation that what is written is what was done, and your approval with the payment record on the final page. It is drafted automatically from the evidence (the worker files one evidence package and writes no paperwork), confirmed by them, reviewed by a person, then delivered to you. Forward it to family, keep it with the house papers, show it to the next contractor.
Can I see the site live before I approve?
Yes. At sign-off you can approve from the evidence package, or book a live video walkthrough: the worker walks the whole site with you on WhatsApp video, Google Meet or Zoom, whichever suits you. You raise anything you see on the spot, the notes are recorded, you confirm them in your portal, and both land on your Completion Report. On builds and larger works we also require a continuous site overview video as part of the evidence itself.
What if I am not happy with the work?
Raise it before you approve. Disputes are reviewed by a person, not an algorithm. The worker gets a fair chance to make it right, and your money stays held until it is resolved.
Do I need to be in Jamaica?
No. Most of our clients are overseas: London, New York, Toronto, anywhere. You review evidence and approve from your phone. That is the whole point.
How are workers vetted? Who is coming to my property?
Nobody attends your property unverified. Before a first job, every worker shows government photo ID on a video call with us, proof of address, and a selfie holding that ID so we know the document is theirs. For work inside occupied homes, or any time a client asks, we require a police record check from the Jamaica Constabulary Force. On top of that: references called, past work reviewed, and a trial job before they are trusted with yours. Certified trades have their certification checked with the issuing body. You are told who is coming, and you see their record before they arrive.
I am a tradesperson. What does it cost to join, and how do I get paid?
Nothing, ever. Joining is free, quoting is free, and you are never charged to be on Yaadly. The client's money is confirmed before you start, so you know it is real. Once your evidence is in and the client approves, you are paid within 3 working days by bank transfer, Lynk wallet or remittance pick up, whichever you chose. You can see what you are owed and what has been paid in your portal, and every completed job builds a record you own.
What kinds of work do you handle?
Repairs and maintenance through to renovations: painting, plumbing, roofing, tiling, masonry, electrical and more. Plus professional services: deposit protection checks, project setup, oversight, contractor sourcing, full project management and property care visits.
