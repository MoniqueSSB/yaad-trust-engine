Client Guidelines v1.0, required to go live

1. What Yaadly does
Yaadly holds your payment for each agreed stage and releases it only when you have seen the evidence and signed it off. Yaadly is not the tradesperson and does not price the work.
2. What you agree to
Describe the job honestly, give safe and timely access to the property, and respond to evidence within five working days. Silence past five days counts as approval.
3. Off-platform payment
Paying a worker directly ends the protection on that stage. We cannot hold or recover money that never reached us.
4. Your details
Your address and phone number stay off the public board. They are shared with one worker only when you choose to share them.
5. Disputes
Where evidence does not match what was agreed in writing, the stage is not released and the job goes to review. Decisions are made on the written scope and the evidence, nothing else.

Say the quiet part first

What these services are

A record of physical works and costs, prepared by a construction project manager. What was observed on site on the date and time stated, and how the prices you were quoted compare against material costs, day rates and completed jobs in our database.

What they are not

Yaadly is not a law firm and nobody here is an attorney-at-law. Nothing is legal advice. We do not verify title, tell you whether a title is good, or advise on what an agreement means, that is an attorney's work and you should instruct one.

We do not carry out land surveys or confirm boundaries, only a surveyor registered under the Land Surveyors Act may do that. We do not certify structural adequacy, only an engineer registered with the PERB may do that. We do not buy, sell, market or introduce property, and we take no commission on any property transaction.

What we owe you

The service carried out with reasonable care and skill, as section 49 of the Consumer Rights Act 2015 requires. Nothing in our terms limits that duty or your rights under it.
