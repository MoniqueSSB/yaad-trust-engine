Privacy policy

What we collect, who we send it to, where in the world they are, and what you can ask us to do about it. Last updated 4 September 2026.

Key points.

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking on this website. That is why there is no cookie banner.
  • We collect what we need to do the work: your details, the property, photographs, and your messages.
  • We never see or store your card number. Stripe handles cards.
  • Identity documents are never sent to any AI model. Blocked in code, with no setting that turns it off.
  • Our text drafting model is Mistral, in the European Union, since 4 September 2026. It used to be in China and this page said so. Details below, not buried.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.
  • You can ask for a copy, a correction or a deletion at any time. Email monique@yaadly.co.uk.
This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. That is why there is no cookie banner. We are not counting you, following you around the web, or selling your attention to anyone. Nothing on these pages needs your consent to load.

What we collect

From clients and from anyone who contacts us:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • The property address, and access details for it.
  • Photographs and video of the property and of work in progress.
  • Voice notes, and the written transcripts made from them.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, by email, or through this website.
  • Payment details, which are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. When you pay for a service by card you are on Stripe's own checkout page, not ours, and what we get back is a confirmation that it went through.

From tradespeople applying to join: a government photo identity document, a live selfie and a short face video, proof of address, tax registration number (TRN), certificates, CV and portfolio.

From tradespeople working on a job, a location, and only ever one they chose to send. There are two moments this can happen and no others: tapping to check in on arrival, which reads the phone's location once, and sending a location on WhatsApp alongside a work update. Both are optional every single time. Declining, having no signal, or simply not doing it never blocks a check-in, never holds up a stage and never delays a payment. It is recorded against that job and that stage, and nothing else. Yaadly does not track anyone. There is no continuous location, no live location, and nothing is read at any moment other than the tap or the share itself. A location makes a job's record harder to argue with, which protects the tradesperson as much as the client, and that is the only reason it is asked for.

Two things we will not soften

One. Our text drafting model is Mistral, in the European Union, since 4 September 2026. Text you send us, meaning job descriptions, messages and voice note transcripts, is processed there when a draft is produced. Until that date this model was MiniMax, in China, and this page said so plainly rather than burying it. It is named here now for the same reason: you are entitled to know where your words went, including after it changes. MiniMax is kept as a fallback. If our Mistral access is ever not configured, or if Mistral refuses a request, whether with a rate limit after we have retried it or by rejecting our access key, that drafting call goes to MiniMax in China, and every such call is written to our own logs so we can say afterwards which messages went there. It is not used while Mistral is configured and answering. Reinstated 5 September 2026, and disclosed here on the same day. Not everything is in the European Union. Photographs of your property are analysed by NVIDIA in the United States, and voice notes are transcribed by Cloudflare with providers in the United States standing behind it. The table below is the full list, and it is the honest one.

Two. Identity documents are never sent to any artificial intelligence model. The government photo identity document, the live selfie and the face video are blocked in our code before they are even downloaded from storage. There is no setting that turns this off. Persona holds the identity check. The supporting paperwork, meaning proof of address, TRN, certificates, CV and portfolio, is reviewed by a model only where the applicant granted consent, and an unanswered question counts as a refusal.
