Cancelling

You buy from Yaadly over WhatsApp, by email, or through this website, so you never meet us face to face when the contract is made. In law that is a distance contract, and it gives you a right to cancel. Last updated 3 September 2026.

In plain English.

  • You have 14 days to cancel, from the day the contract is made. No reason needed.
  • To cancel, send us a message saying so. Email, WhatsApp or the contact form. Any of them is enough, and it counts from the day you send it.
  • If you never asked us to start early, you pay nothing.
  • If you did ask us to start early, you pay a fair share for the work already done, and no more.
  • Refunds go back to the same card or account, within 14 days. No fee for cancelling.

The rest of this page is the same thing said properly.

You have 14 days to cancel, counted from the day the contract is made. You do not have to give a reason. This right comes from the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, and it applies to consumers, not to businesses buying for business purposes.

If you ask us to start inside the 14 days

Most people want the work to start straight away, which usually means starting before the 14 days have run out. We can only do that if you expressly ask us to, and we will ask you for that in writing at booking, alongside this cancellation information.

If you then cancel part way through, you pay a proportionate amount for the work already done, and no more. If you never asked us to start early, or we never gave you this cancellation information, you pay nothing at all.

Two clocks, and they are not the same clock

Where we authorise your card at booking, that authorisation lasts seven days. Your right to cancel lasts fourteen. Charging your card on day five, when you accept the work, does not end your right to cancel: it still has nine days left to run. If you cancel inside those nine days, the money comes back to you, less a proportionate amount for work already done if you asked us to start early.

The Oversight Retainer works differently, because it is a monthly subscription rather than an authorisation. The first month is charged when you start. The same fourteen days to cancel apply from that day, and stopping the subscription after that takes 30 days notice.

On a job paid by invoice and bank transfer, the same fourteen days apply from the day the contract is made, and a refund goes back to the account the transfer came from.

Refunds

We refund to the same card or account you paid from, within 14 days of you telling us you are cancelling. There is no fee for cancelling. What each service costs is on the services page, and the route your money takes is on the payments page.
