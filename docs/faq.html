Questions and answers

Asked before, answered plainly.

If yours is not here, ask it. A person reads every message and replies within one working day.

How do I start a job?

Two ways, both free. Post the job, where it saves as a draft before anything personal is asked and no account is needed to get a quote. Or message us on WhatsApp with where the property is and what needs doing, in text, photos or a voice note, Patois or English, and we reply within 24 hours. No app to download either way.

Where do you operate?

Yaadly is built for property work across Jamaica. We are launching in Kingston and Portmore first, with professional oversight on every job. Starting deep in one area is deliberate: it is how we keep every job verified rather than spreading thin. More parishes open as we grow. Elsewhere in Jamaica today? Message us anyway: professional services travel further than repairs, and you will be first to know when we reach your parish.

How does the money side work?

The tradesperson is not paid for a stage until you approve the evidence. You see timestamped proof before anyone is paid, and nothing moves without your say so. Today you pay your tradesperson directly, stage by stage, against an agreement you both sign before work starts, and Yaadly's own fee is invoiced separately after the work. Online payment through Yaadly opens shortly, and we will say so plainly when it does. The full route your money takes is written down on the payments page.

What does it cost?

Professional services are priced on the page. Every one shows the full price and the founding rate side by side, because a discount off a price nobody can actually buy is a misleading action under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024. Deposit Protection Check from £149, Visual Check from £95 a visit, Condition Report from £245, Technical Sign-off from £245, Oversight Retainer from £495 a month, Property Care from £75 a visit, Full Project Management 12 to 15% of build cost. Founding rates are limited to the first five clients per service. See the full list.

Property work through Yaadly gets a written quote benchmarked against real material costs and real day rates, confirmed before any money moves. No surprises after the fact, and no "farrin price": a client overseas pays what a client in Jamaica pays.

What is inside the price of a job?

The quote you accept is the whole price. It is itemised, labour split from materials, and materials are never marked up: they are a pass-through at cost, with the receipt filed against your job. The labour price includes what Yaadly does around the work: vetting the tradesperson, the written scope, checking the evidence against it, a dispute review by a person if you raise one, and somebody to call who answers.

The rest, plainly. On very small jobs a minimum applies, J$3,500 to J$4,500 depending on the trade, and it is on the quote before you accept. If we advance cash for materials there is a disclosed 5% admin charge. If you pay from abroad, the exchange rate we use carries a margin of about 2%; paying from Jamaica in J$ skips that entirely. Every charge that applies to your job is on your quote, itemised, before you agree to anything.

What do I actually get at the end of a job?

A signed Completion Report, as a PDF that is yours to keep: the job summary, before and after photographs side by side, the verification record, the worker's own confirmation that what is written is what was done, and your approval with the payment record on the final page. It is drafted automatically from the evidence, confirmed by the worker, reviewed by a person, then delivered to you. Forward it to family, keep it with the house papers, show it to the next contractor.

Can I see the site live before I approve?

Yes. At sign-off you can approve from the evidence package, or book a live video walkthrough: the worker walks the whole site with you on WhatsApp video, Google Meet or Zoom, whichever suits you. You raise anything you see on the spot, the notes are recorded, you confirm them in your portal, and both land on your Completion Report. On builds and larger works we also require a continuous site overview video as part of the evidence itself.

What if I am not happy with the work?

Raise it before you approve. Disputes are reviewed by a person, not an algorithm. The worker gets a fair chance to make it right, and nothing more is paid while the dispute is open.

Do I need to be in Jamaica?

No. Most of our clients are overseas: London, New York, Toronto, anywhere. You review evidence and approve from your phone. That is the whole point.

How are workers vetted? Who is coming to my property?

Nobody attends your property unverified. Before a first job, every worker passes an identity check run by an independent verification provider: government photo ID checked for authenticity, with a live selfie and liveness detection, so the person holding the document is the person on it. They show their past work, and their references are checked.

A worker cannot take on any job over £500, any work inside an occupied home, or any job where they hold keys, until their three referees have been spoken to directly. That line is drawn at £500 because that is roughly where a job stops being a call-out and starts being someone's savings. Certified trades have their certification checked with the body that issued it. You are told who is coming before they arrive.

And the standard holds afterwards: we only keep working with people who meet the evidence standard. Fall short once and we stop sending work.

I am a tradesperson. What does it cost to join, and how do I get paid?

Joining is free, quoting is free, and you are never charged for a lead, win or lose. Your price is agreed per job, in writing, before you start, and it never touches your materials: those are paid at cost against your receipt. Once your evidence is in and the client approves, you are paid within 3 working days by bank transfer, Lynk wallet or remittance pick up, whichever you chose. You can see what you are owed and what has been paid in your portal, and every completed job builds a record you own and can take anywhere.

Two ways to join, and the chat is the quicker one. Apply on the site, or start it on WhatsApp and answer five short questions in the chat, Patois or English, with a photo of a finished job. Your ID check comes back to you on WhatsApp too.

What kinds of work do you handle?

Repairs and maintenance through to renovations: painting, plumbing, roofing, tiling, masonry, electrical, septic and drainage, grilles and gates, and more. Plus professional services: deposit protection checks, visual checks, condition reports, technical sign-off, monthly oversight, full project management and property care visits.
