The payment journey

Where your money goes,
and who stands behind it.

Standing behind the work until it is proven is what Yaadly is built to do. It is not switched on yet. We are waiting on legal sign off and insurance, and will not take a client's payment until both are in hand.

Until then, you pay your tradesperson directly, stage by stage, under an agreement we write for you: never more than 25% up front, materials at cost, payments tied to milestones you can see, nothing final until you have inspected the work. Yaadly's own fee for vetting and overseeing the job is invoiced to you separately, and it is never taken out of what your tradesperson was promised.

Once live, you pay Yaadly instead, one price that already includes what Yaadly keeps for vetting the tradesperson and standing behind the outcome. That price is built from a plain formula: the job's real cost, plus a 15% Guarantee and Support fee for the vetting, the evidence chain and the dispute service. The tradesperson's own price is agreed with him directly before the job starts, and typically nets to around 88% of the labour value once that is set. Nothing is added afterwards, nothing taken from what he was promised. If the work is wrong, that is ours to put right, not yours to chase him for.

If anyone sends you a payment link for a tradesperson's labour or materials claiming it is from Yaadly, it is not.

Paying for a report or a retainer

How you pay us, and how we know it landed.

By bank transfer, against an invoice we send you, in whichever of GBP, USD or CAD suits you. We do not take card details over the phone or in a message, and we do not send a payment link.

The account details on that invoice are the only ones we will ever give you. We will never change them by phone, email or WhatsApp part way through a job. If a message asks you to pay a different account, do not pay it. Check with us first, through this site's contact form or the WhatsApp number you already have for us, not by replying to the message that asked.

Reports under £200 are paid in full before we start. £200 and over is half before we start and half on delivery. The Oversight Retainer is billed monthly, and you can cancel with 30 days notice.

We check the account ourselves and start work once the payment has actually arrived, not once it has been sent. That is usually within one working day.

Cancellation and refund terms for these reports are being finalised with legal advice, and will be added here before they are needed.
