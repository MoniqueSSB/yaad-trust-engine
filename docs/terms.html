Terms of service

The parts that are settled are set out in full below. The parts that are still with our adviser say so, plainly, instead of being filled with wording nobody has checked. Last updated 5 September 2026.

What this means for you, in plain English.

  • If you are a client on a managed job: you are buying the work from Yaadly, not from the tradesperson. You pay Yaadly. If the work is wrong, you come to us and it is ours to put right.
  • If you are a client on an oversight job: your contractor stays yours and you pay them directly. Yaadly holds none of that money. What you buy from us is the inspection and the report.
  • If you are a tradesperson: you are Yaadly's subcontractor on a managed job, not the client's. Your price is agreed with us in writing before you start, and Yaadly pays you, not the client.
  • Either way: every price is agreed in writing before anything starts, and a named person at Yaadly checks the work before anyone is paid.

Who you contract with

Yaadly Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17358077. A UK registered company, not an individual and not an introduction service.

What we supply

On a managed job, Yaadly supplies the work. We price it, and we subcontract an identity checked local tradesperson to carry it out. You do not contract with the tradesperson and you do not pay the tradesperson. If the work is wrong, you come to us, and putting it right is ours to sort out, not yours to chase him for.

On an oversight engagement, Yaadly supplies the eyes and nothing else. Your contractor remains your contractor, engaged by you on your own terms, and you pay them directly. Yaadly holds none of the money for that building work and is not responsible for that contractor's work. What Yaadly supplies, and is responsible for, is attending, recording against the agreed checklist, and writing it up. Our fee for that is invoiced separately.

On a check or report service, such as a Deposit Protection Check, a Visual Check, a Condition Report or a Technical Sign-off, Yaadly supplies the report. We do not carry out the building work being assessed on those jobs, and we do not pay whoever does.

Yaadly is not a substitute for a structural engineer, an attorney, a quantity surveyor or a land surveyor. Where a question belongs to one of those, we refer it out to a named professional in writing rather than answering it ourselves.

Price

Every price is fixed and agreed in writing before anything starts. It does not move afterwards without your agreement in writing. Our service prices are published on the services page, and our research on what building work costs in Jamaica is on the prices page.

Payment

Payment terms are agreed in writing for each job, before anything starts. Different jobs need different terms, so there is no single rule, and we are not going to pretend there is one. What governs your job is the written terms agreed with that job.

Above £500, the job is invoiced. The invoice says plainly that you bought the job from Yaadly Ltd, and it is refundable in full until you accept the work.

At or under £500, your card is authorised at booking and charged once the work is done and you have accepted it, not before. The price of the job decides which of the two applies. Everything else, the stages and what each one covers, is agreed in writing with that job.

Yaadly does not operate an escrow service and does not hold money on your behalf. On a managed job, Yaadly is paid for the work and pays its subcontractor out of that. On an oversight engagement, Yaadly is paid only its own fee.

Card payment is not switched on yet, so until it is, every job is invoiced whatever its size, and paid by bank transfer in GBP, USD or CAD. Full detail is on the payments page.

The account details on our invoice are the only ones we will ever give you. We will never change them by phone, email or WhatsApp part way through a job.

Evidence, checking and sign off

Every stage is documented with timestamped evidence before anything is paid: arrival on site, before photographs, materials receipts, after photographs, and a walk-round video at the end. The person filing a photograph says at the time whether it is the before or the after, so the pair is marked on the record rather than left for you to work out from the captions. You see all of it, and you use it to decide whether to accept the work.

The evidence is how the work is confirmed. It is not a mechanism that pays anybody. On a managed job, Yaadly checks the evidence against the agreed scope and then pays its subcontractor. That is Yaadly’s decision and Yaadly’s liability, not yours.

A named person signs off every stage and approves every payment. Never an automatic timer. Approving a stage means a real, recorded person looked at the evidence and decided. Nothing moves because a countdown ran out, a confidence score passed, or a check went green on its own, and there is no setting that makes it work that way. Your approval closes the stage with Yaadly; Yaadly pays the tradesperson under its own separate agreement with them, so they are never left waiting on you.

Cancelling

You have 14 days to cancel, from the day the contract is made. How to do it, and what happens if you asked us to start early, is set out on the cancelling page.

Complaints

Raise it before you accept the work if you can. Write to monique@yaadly.co.uk or send us a message on WhatsApp. A person reads it, not a queue. We will acknowledge within one working day, tell you who is dealing with it, and come back to you with an answer and what we propose to do. Complaints are reviewed by a person, never by an algorithm. The tradesperson gets a fair chance to make it right, and nothing further is paid out on the job while the complaint is open. If you are not satisfied with that answer, say so and it will be looked at again by us before you need to go anywhere else.
