Legal

Terms of service

The parts that are settled are set out in full below. The parts that are still with our adviser say so, plainly, instead of being filled with wording nobody has checked. Last updated 9 September 2026.

What this means for you, in plain English.

  • If you are a client: on a job you are buying the work from Yaadly, not from the tradesperson. On a professional service you are buying it from the founder. You pay Yaadly. If the work is wrong, you come to us and it is ours to put right.
  • If you are a tradesperson: you are Yaadly's subcontractor on every job, not the client's. Your price is agreed with us in writing before you start, and Yaadly pays you, not the client. Your price includes your tools, consumables, transport and waste: Yaadly does not reimburse a broken blade, a spoiled bag of cement or materials over-ordered. You set your own hours to meet the agreed stages, and work that fails the check is put right at your own cost before that stage is paid.
  • Either way: every price is agreed in writing before anything starts, and a named person at Yaadly checks the work before anyone is paid.

Who you contract with

It depends on what you buy. Professional services (the Visual Check, one visit or the monthly plan, and the Before You Pay Check) are supplied by the founder of Yaadly, Monique Sewell-Bennett, as a sole trader, 55 Remington Road, London N15 6SS. Jobs, Managed Work and business services are supplied by Yaadly Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17358077. Your receipt or invoice names which one you bought from.

What we supply

On a job, Yaadly supplies the work. We price it, and we subcontract an identity checked local tradesperson to carry it out. You do not contract with the tradesperson and you do not pay the tradesperson. If the work is wrong, you come to us, and putting it right is ours to sort out, not yours to chase him for.

On a professional service, such as a Visual Check or a Before You Pay Check, the founder supplies the report, and you buy it from her at the published price. Any property work you then commission through Yaadly is a job, and the paragraph above applies to it.

Yaadly is not a substitute for a structural engineer, an attorney, a quantity surveyor or a land surveyor. Where a question belongs to one of those, we refer it out to a named professional in writing rather than answering it ourselves.

Price

Every price is fixed and agreed in writing before anything starts. It does not move afterwards without your agreement in writing. Our service prices are published on the services page, and our research on what building work costs in Jamaica is on the prices page.

Payment

Payment terms are agreed in writing for each job, before anything starts. Different jobs need different terms, so there is no single rule, and we are not going to pretend there is one. What governs your job is the written terms agreed with that job.

Every job is invoiced by Yaadly Ltd. The invoice says plainly that you bought the job from Yaadly Ltd. You pay it by bank transfer or by card in your portal, and a card is charged when you pay, not held. A short job is paid in full when it is booked; a larger job is paid stage by stage. The stages and what each one covers are agreed in writing with that job.

If the site turns out different from what was described, a rock shelf under the soil, a wall that crumbles when it is opened up, the fixed price does not quietly stretch. The extra work is written up as an add-on with its own price and its own evidence, and nothing is done until you have agreed it in your portal. You never pay for an add-on you did not agree to, and the tradesperson is never asked to absorb work that was not in the scope.

Materials. On a job Yaadly buys the materials from the merchant and pays the merchant directly. The tradesperson is paid for labour only and never handles money for materials.

If you cancel before you accept the work, you get your money back less materials already bought for your job and work already done to the agreed scope, and that only if you asked us to start inside your 14 days. If nothing was bought and nothing was done, you get everything back. See the cancellation page. This wording is being reviewed by our adviser.

Exchange rates. Your price is fixed in your own currency when you accept the quote, and a quote holds for 14 days. If the rate moves after you accept, Yaadly carries the difference, not you and not the tradesperson.

Yaadly does not operate an escrow service and does not hold money on your behalf. On a job, Yaadly is paid for the work and pays its subcontractor out of that. On a check, a report or a retainer, the founder is paid the published price of that service.

Card payment is live for the one-off fixed-fee services, all of which are under £500. Cards are handled by Stripe and we never see or store your card number. A card authorisation lasts seven days; if the visit falls later it lapses without anything being taken and we send a fresh link. The monthly Visual Check plan is agreed with you in writing before the first month is billed, on the terms set out on the services page: one visit a month, a missed visit rebooked within seven days or that month refunded, and 30 days notice to stop with no minimum term. An invoice paid by bank transfer in GBP, USD or CAD remains available on any job at any size if you prefer it. Full detail is on the payments page.

You will only ever pay one of two payees: the founder, Monique Sewell-Bennett, for a professional service, or Yaadly Ltd for a job. A card payment to the founder shows as Yaadly Founder, the name of her Stripe account. The details on your invoice are the only ones we will ever give you. We will never change them by phone, email or WhatsApp part way through a job.

Evidence, checking and sign off

Every stage is documented with timestamped evidence before anything is paid: arrival on site, before photographs, materials receipts, after photographs, and a walk-round video at the end. The person filing a photograph says at the time which part of the job it belongs to, so you read it in sections rather than working it out from the captions: before, during the work, problems found on site, after, and materials on site. You see all of it, and you use it to decide whether to accept the work.

Anything found on site that was not part of what you agreed is filed as a problem, and you see it in its own section. If putting it right changes the price or the timeline, that is agreed with you in writing before the work happens, never added afterwards.

The evidence is how the work is confirmed. It is not a mechanism that pays anybody. Yaadly checks the evidence against the agreed scope and then pays its subcontractor. That is Yaadly’s decision and Yaadly’s liability, not yours.

A named person signs off every stage and approves every payment. Never an automatic timer. Approving a stage means a real, recorded person looked at the evidence and decided. Nothing moves because a countdown ran out, a confidence score passed, or a check went green on its own, and there is no setting that makes it work that way. Your acceptance comes first, then a named person at Yaadly approves the stage, and the tradesperson is paid within 7 days of that approval under their own separate agreement with Yaadly. If you go quiet, we remind you twice, and after seven days with no answer a named person decides on the evidence. Never a timer.

Cancelling

You have 14 days to cancel, from the day the contract is made. How to do it, and what happens if you asked us to start early, is set out on the cancelling page.

Complaints

Raise it before you accept the work if you can. Write to monique@yaadly.co.uk or send us a message on WhatsApp. A person reads it, not a queue. We will acknowledge within one working day and tell you who is dealing with it. The tradesperson has 48 hours to put it right. Within five working days of your complaint we come back to you with an answer and what we propose to do. Complaints are reviewed by a person, never by an algorithm. The tradesperson gets a fair chance to make it right, and nothing further is paid out on the job while the complaint is open. If you are not satisfied with that answer, say so and it will be looked at again by a different named person at Yaadly, not the one who gave the first answer. You can also go outside us at any point: in Jamaica, to the Consumer Affairs Commission, and in the UK, to Citizens Advice.
