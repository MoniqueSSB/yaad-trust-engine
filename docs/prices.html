What things actually cost

The price nobody publishes.

Property work in Jamaica is priced privately, in WhatsApp messages, one owner at a time. That is exactly how an overseas owner ends up quoted more than the neighbour for the same job. This page is what we found when we went looking for a public number, researched directly from government tiers, vendor prices and dated rate cards, not invented and not smoothed over. Every quote we review is measured against these numbers, and anything added on top, our fee or a cost that genuinely comes with the work, is shown as its own line rather than folded into the price.

Researched 1 August 2026 · exchange rate used throughout £1 ≈ J$210, US$1 ≈ J$158 · refreshed quarterly, or sooner where noted.

What this page is. Research into what building work costs in Jamaica on the open market, so you have something to check a quote against. It is not a Yaadly price list. Yaadly’s own fixed fees are on the services page, and what Yaadly charges on a job is further down this page.
The honest headline first: for several of the most common jobs, we could find no published price in Jamaica. Not on a government site, not from a trade body, not from a single vendor we could find. Painting, a bathroom renovation, septic work and a block wall by the foot are all priced privately, job by job, with nothing to check a quote against. That gap is the actual problem this page, and Yaadly itself, exists to close. Where a real number exists below, it is shown with its source and how confident it is. Where it does not, that is stated plainly rather than guessed at.

Where the data runs out

Painting, bathroom renovation, septic work and a block wall priced by the foot: we found no published price for any of them when this page was researched. Four separate research passes looked, on 1 August 2026, and they covered these four jobs, not every trade. Every price for this work happens in a private WhatsApp message between one owner and one tradesperson, which is precisely the arrangement that lets an overseas owner be quoted more than a neighbour would pay for identical work, with nothing to check it against.

This is not a gap we are filling with a guess. It is the reason a benchmark like this one has to be built from job data rather than found on a shelf: every quote reviewed through Yaadly from here adds a real, dated data point to it.

What this page is, and is not

These are observed market ranges from research, dated and sourced. They are not a quote for your job, and nobody at Yaadly prices a job from this table alone.

What a real job costs depends on scope, access, condition and the materials chosen, none of which a price guide can see. When you post a job through Yaadly, you get a written, itemised quote, labour split from materials, checked against numbers like the ones on this page before you are asked to agree to anything. That check is a person's judgement, not an automated estimate: Yaadly does not price your job, it tells you honestly whether the price you were given looks right.
