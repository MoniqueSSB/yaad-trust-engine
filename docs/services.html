Nothing is charged now.

Every fixed-fee service on this page can be paid by card, and the card button appears here the moment your booking is registered. Nothing is taken at that point: on a one-off service your card is authorised and only charged once the work is done and you have accepted it. You have 14 days to cancel.

Leave this unticked and nothing starts until your 14 days have run out. Your right to cancel lasts the full 14 days either way.
How you pay: payment terms are agreed in writing for each job, before anything starts. Above £500 the job is invoiced and paid by bank transfer in GBP, USD or CAD. At or under £500, which is every fixed-fee service on this page, you can pay by card: your card is authorised at booking and charged once the work is done and accepted. The Oversight Retainer is a monthly card subscription instead, billed from the day you start, with 30 days notice to stop. You have 14 days to cancel. Yaadly does not operate an escrow service and does not hold money on your behalf. Full payment terms.
Inspection and oversight, not a guarantee. Yaadly records condition, judges workmanship against the standard for that trade, and oversees progress. Yaadly does not guarantee an outcome, and four things always go out to a named professional in writing rather than being guessed at: valuation to a licensed valuer, title and legal opinion to an attorney, structural engineering to a PERB registered engineer, and boundaries to a commissioned land surveyor.
