What you get
One visit a month by a local checker, the same fixed checklist every time, a timestamped photo set, and anything that has changed since the last visit flagged. We record what we see. We do not rate or advise.
Price
£100 a month for one visit a month. More visits a month are quoted on request.
Where
Kingston and Portmore for now.
When
We agree a week with you each month and confirm the day at least 48 hours before. Your photos follow within two working days of the visit.
Access
You arrange it, through a key holder or someone at the house. If the checker arrives at the agreed time and cannot get in, that visit counts, and we offer another day that month if we can.
If we miss a visit
We rebook it within seven days. If we cannot, that month's fee is refunded in full.
Paying
Billed monthly in advance, from the month of your first visit. The first visit is booked once your first month's payment has cleared.
Stopping
No minimum term. Give 30 days notice at any time. Your 14 day right to cancel applies from the day you sign up.
Places
Limited each month. If we are full, we tell you before you pay anything.
