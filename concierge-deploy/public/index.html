Pay workers

Every pay invoice Yaadly owes a worker, raised when a stage is approved. Each row links to the pay invoice, the job's invoices and the job's record of who approved the work, and shows whether the client has paid for it. Nobody is paid until the client's bill for that work is marked paid: the buttons stay grey and the database refuses, for Wise and Stripe alike. First call the worker back to check their bank details and press Call-back done; the database refuses Mark as sent until you have, and again whenever they change their details. Then either pay it from Wise, to the recipient saved there, and press Mark as sent; or, for a worker who has set up Stripe, press Pay with Stripe: it shows you Stripe's fee, exchange rate and what lands in J$, and sends only when you confirm. Either way the invoice is marked paid, stamped with you and the time, once, and the worker gets a WhatsApp. Yaadly stores no worker bank details, and a worker should never send them to the Yaadly WhatsApp number.

Materials tranches

The materials line of an accepted quote, paid to the worker so they can buy the goods, once the client has paid for it, and never fee'd on either side. Releasing records the decision and moves no money: you then pay the worker from your business bank app and press Mark as sent, which is when the worker gets a WhatsApp. Yaadly stores no worker bank details. The receipt comes back afterwards and is recorded here against the release it accounts for. The database refuses a tranche until the client has said where materials are kept (Materials store, on the jobs view), and refuses any total above the quoted materials line. Every release is stamped against you.
