# Yaadly job taxonomy, all 18 trades

Drafted 26 Aug 2026. This is the list behind every dropdown on the job card. It is what makes two roofing jobs on the board comparable.

**How to read it.** *Stages* is how many times money moves and evidence is required. *Size bands* are what the client picks, never a price, just enough for a worker to know whether to bother. *Evidence* is the default checklist for that job type; a client can add or drop items, but never below what the trade needs to be provable.


## Plumbing

| Job type | Stages | Size bands |
|---|---|---|
| Leak trace and repair | 1 | One fitting or joint · One room affected · Two to three rooms · Whole property · Not sure, worker to assess |
| Blocked drain or waste | 1 | One fixture · One branch run · Whole house run · Main line out to the road · Not sure, worker to assess |
| Bathroom or kitchen fit-out | 3 | One fixture swapped · Full bathroom · Full kitchen · Two or more rooms |
| Re-pipe, part or whole | 5 | One run · One floor · Whole house · Not sure, worker to assess |
| Water heater | 1 | Repair the existing one · Replace like for like · New install and pipe run |
| Pressure or flow problem | 1 | *generic, half day / 1 to 2 days / 3+ days / not sure* |

**Default evidence**

- Arrival photos, geotagged
- The leak or fault before work
- Isolation valve shown closed
- Pipe or fitting removed, laid out
- New work before it is boxed in
- Pressure and flow tested at a tap, on video
- The finished work, tidied down
- Walk-round clip
