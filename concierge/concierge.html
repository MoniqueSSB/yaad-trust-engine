Job Invoices

One row per booked job. Raises the client's single all-in invoice: the labour price, Yaadly's 15% Guarantee & Support fee, and materials at cost. The client buys the whole job from Yaadly and is never billed for the worker. What Yaadly owes the tradesperson, their quoted labour less the agreed 12% plus materials, is a separate payable raised once the job is complete.

Materials tranches

The materials line of an accepted quote, paid to the worker against a receipt before any labour stage, and never fee'd on either side.
