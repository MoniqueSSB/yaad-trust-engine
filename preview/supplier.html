Materials

The materials line of an accepted quote, once the client has paid for it, and never fee'd on either side. Each job buys one of two ways, set on the job as materials by. Worker buys: exactly the lane that is live today, money released to the worker, receipt comes back afterwards. Yaadly buys from a supplier: the same release, made out to a supplier on account instead of the worker. The supplier confirms, the tradesperson collects with a code, the tradesperson films the goods in the client's nominated store (the materials evidence that already exists), and only then is the supplier's invoice paid, from Pay workers and suppliers, with the same call-back-first rule. The database refuses either lane until the client has said where materials are kept, and refuses any total above what the client has paid. Every release is stamped against you.

⚖️ The governing rule holds here: releasing records a decision and moves no money. The supplier moves goods, never money. Matching the goods to the site evidence and marking an invoice paid are your clicks, one named person, every time. Nothing is paid on a timer, on a supplier's say so, or because the evidence looks fine to a model.

What this adds to what Yaadly already has

The method statement. Nothing below is built yet. The point of this page is to agree the shape before a line of it is.

What already exists and is reused as is

  • The nominated materials store on the job (jobs.materials_store, nominate_materials_store()). Still the first gate. No store, no release, either lane.
  • The release (materials_releases, release_materials_tranche()): refuses more than the quote and more than the client has paid. A supplier order is this row, made out to a supplier.
  • Sent and receipt (mark_materials_sent, record_materials_receipt()): sent becomes the payment to the supplier; receipt_ref holds the supplier's invoice number.
  • Materials evidence (evidence kind materials, filed by the tradesperson from the job room): this is the match. No new "delivery confirmed" concept.
  • The portal door (web/app/portal/(gated)) and the way the worker portal reads only its own rows through RLS, never through a filter in the page.
  • Conversations on the desk and yaad-desk-reply for answering. Supplier messages are threads, not a new enquiries table.
  • Pay workers and mark_worker_paid(): call-back first, then pay from the bank app, then record it. Same screen, one more payee type.

What is added, in order of size

  • One table: suppliers (name, parish, contact, account reference, the signed-in user, created by). RLS: a supplier reads their own row; admins read all.
  • Columns on materials_releases: payee ('worker' or 'supplier', default 'worker' so every existing row is unchanged), supplier_id, collection_code, confirmed_at, ready_at, collected_at, and a lines JSON for the order lines. RLS: a supplier reads releases where supplier_id is theirs, through a view that carries job id, title, parish and stage and no client column.
  • One value on jobs.materials_by: 'yaadly', meaning Yaadly buys from a supplier. Set on the job by the desk, like the store.
  • One value on invoices.payable_to: 'supplier', and mark_supplier_paid() shaped exactly like mark_worker_paid().
  • Three supplier functions, each touching only a release whose supplier_id is the caller's: confirm, mark ready, mark collected (takes the code, refuses a wrong one). None of them touches money or evidence.
  • Desk: the Materials view reads payee and shows the lane; a Suppliers view in People; Pay workers lists supplier invoices too.
  • App: /portal/supplier, one page plus a job room, in the worker portal's components.
  • Site terms: already say Yaadly buys from the merchant (7 October). Nothing to change there; this is what makes the sentence true.

Decisions that are yours, not the build's

  • Payment terms to suppliers. 7 days after the site evidence is the placeholder, matching the tradesperson terms. Nothing on this page settles it.
  • Address on a delivery order. A supplier delivering to site needs the address; a supplier the tradesperson collects from does not. This page shows it on the delivery note only. Data protection question, your call.
  • Which lane is the default. Worker buys stays live and untouched. Whether new jobs default to the supplier lane, or it is chosen job by job, is a desk setting you decide.
  • Returns, shortfalls and price rises between confirmation and collection. Shown here as a held release and a conversation. Not ruled on by this page.
  • Family and friends. Guardrail 4 keeps them out of the supply chain. A supplier is supply. It applies.

What does not change

Release, match and pay are each a named person's click. The supplier never sees a client's name or contact. The tradesperson never handles materials money. Materials are passed to the client at the supplier's price with no fee on either side. The engine stays stateless and pricing stays a lookup; nothing here goes near either.
